System 11 · Core mechanics

Signature

Everything emits on five channels. Stealth and detection, in the open and in the matrix, are one mechanic: signature moves the difficulty.

The principle

Everything emits. Nothing is hidden, only quiet.

Every character, drone, vehicle and item has a Signature on each of five channels, 0 to 8. Detection is an ordinary task roll. Signature on the channel used moves the difficulty in the detector's favour, point for point. There is no stealth score and no detection score; there is a sum of what you are emitting and a roll against it.

  • Signature is a derived score: a baseline from the body, permanent additions from gear, and temporary additions from states and actions. Every part has an origin, so describe shows why you are loud.
  • An observer needs a Sense on a channel to detect on it. Eyes and ears are given. Thermal, EM and Data senses come from gear.
  • Reducing signature is gear's effect job. Providing a sense is gear's gate job. Chrome adds signature. The gear catalogue for stealth falls out of the three jobs already written.
Why signature and not a stealth stat

A single stealth number hides every decision inside it. Five channels make the decisions visible: turn the commlink off, wear the shielding jacket, send the unaugmented face past the scanner, don't fire the rifle. The heavily augmented character is quiet-footed and loud on a scanner, which is a real trade-off and a cleaner cyberpunk statement than a humanity score. It is also one mechanic for a warehouse and a matrix node, where most systems bolt on a second ruleset.

Why Data and not radiation

The five channels come from a video-game model where radiation mattered. Here it does not, and the matrix does. Giving the matrix a channel means a decker sneaking through a node and a samurai sneaking through a warehouse run the same rule, with ICE in the role of the guard.

Resolution

Whoever acts rolls. Signature counts for the detector either way.

Searching: Notice against the target's Concealment on the channel: 11 + Stealth − Signature, plus range. Sneaking: Stealth against the observer's Passive Notice on their best channel: 11 + Notice + Signature, plus range.

  • Concealment per channel is a derived score on the sneaking character's sheet, spoken like Defence: "Concealment 7 on EM, 10 on acoustic".
  • Range uses the same chips as shooting: Short 0, Medium +2, Long +4, Extreme +6 against detection. A sensor defines its bands as a weapon does.
  • Pace applies. A Careful search takes twice as long and cannot go badly. A Methodical sweep of a room is certain to find anything whose Concealment is 11 + Notice or less. A Rushed glance is a d20.
  • The certainty rule applies and is the point. A sheet that says "you will be detected on EM" before anyone rolls is what turns a heist into planning.

Why signature subtracts from the number spoken

Defence is 11 + rating, and signature could have been a chip on the observer's roll instead. Folding it into Concealment keeps one spoken number per channel and lets the certainty rule read it directly. The chips stay for what the observer can see about the situation, range above all.

  • The sneaker's bands are the mirror: a bad failure on Stealth is the alarm; a failure is "the guard looks your way", the cue to hold still.
  • Holding still is a declared non-action. Acoustic drops to 0 and Optical by 1 for the turn. Temporary signature from earlier actions still runs its lifespan: the gunshot is heard whether or not you freeze afterwards.
  • Alert is a scene condition on the scene card. An alert observer searches at Careful pace for free, because it is all they are doing.
  • Detection by a system is an extended task on the system's side where it matters: a trace, a camera sweep, a patrol. Each interval the system rolls; the sneaker's progress is staying under its Concealment.

The matrix is the same thing

  • A deck online has Data signature: baseline for the deck, plus programs running, plus actions this turn. Stealth programs subtract. Attack programs add when used.
  • ICE and nodes have a Data sense with Notice equal to the node rating. They roll against the decker's Concealment on Data: 11 + Hacking − Data signature.
  • A trace is an extended task the ICE runs against the decker. Its progress per interval comes from its Notice rolls; the decker's bad failures hand it progress for free.
  • Data signature is why a Rushed hack is tempting and dangerous: +2 Data this turn, and the d20.
  • Jacking in also raises EM by 1 in the room the decker is sitting in. The samurai guarding them is standing next to a beacon.

What it needs from the rest

  • Character: Concealment per channel and Senses join the derived scores.
  • Items: every item carries its signature per channel and any sense it provides.
  • Targets: an observer's sentence gains a channel: "Guard, Notice 3, has thermal". The range chips are reused.
  • Engine: a mod carries a channel tag so the five sums can be calculated and described. No other change.
Open: a sixth channel for being memorable

How conspicuous a person is in a crowd (visible chrome, dress, bearing, a face on a watch list) behaves like signature against Streetwise and a guard's suspicion. It may be Optical with social chips, or its own channel, Profile. Decide with the social rules.
