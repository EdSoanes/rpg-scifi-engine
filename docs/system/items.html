Items

One schema for every piece of gear: item, slot, component. How gear reaches the sheet as mods, actions and costs; feeds, tags, condition, and worked builds.

One schema

Item → slot → component. Three verbs.

A pistol, a cyberdeck, a cyberarm, a medkit and a car are all an item with slots that hold components. Everything any of them does reaches the sheet as one of the engine's three verbs: a mod on a property, an action granted, or a cost on use.

  • A component can itself have slots. A deck hosts programs; a cyberarm hosts a hidden holster that hosts a pistol. The engine's child objects already allow this.
  • An item with all its components is one object, which is what a handover card carries.
  • Items are authored from templates, one per item kind, so the content tools work from the same schema.
Why one schema

A separate model for weapons, armour, decks and implants means four authoring forms, four sheet layouts and four places to fix the same bug. One schema means a new kind of gear is a template, not a feature. It is also what makes transparency free: every mod has the component and item as its origin, so the sheet already explains "+1 to hit, smartlink, needs link, provided by cyber-eyes" without anyone writing that sentence.

Where this came from

The shape is adapted from a video-game technology model with the physics removed. That model resolves watts, heat and pressure every tick; a table does not. What survives is the hierarchy, the idea that a component plugs into what the item provides without knowing about other components, the condition ladder with wear and damage as separate channels, and the principle that nothing fails invisibly. What went is efficiency, buses as quantities, materials, pressure windows and resistance tables. Every value here is a whole number, a dice expression or a word, because those are what a sheet shows and a player types.

Mismatches

Every mismatch has a named consequence

The engine never refuses, so a wrong component seats and a required slot can be left empty. The item reports the mismatch in words and names what happens.

Why report and not refuse

The source model refuses a wrong plug physically, because in a 3D world a plug that does not go in is a fact the player can see. This engine's rule is that constraints are known but never enforced, so the equivalent fact is a sentence on the sheet and a consequence the player accepted. Jury-rigging is allowed and costs something visible.

Condition

Condition ladder
StageEffectSignature
NominalAs written.As written.
DegradedEffect values one step down. Malfunction on a flat d10 of 1 per use.Powered: Thermal +1, Acoustic +1.
FailedMods and actions do not count. Shown as set aside.Powered: EM +1 (fault beacon).
DestroyedGone. Components may survive to be salvaged.None.
  • Two channels move the ladder. Wear comes from use and is fixed by maintenance. Damage comes from hits and is fixed by repair. Both fixes are extended tasks (Electronics or the relevant skill), with the item's complexity as the work total and tolerance.
  • Wear ticks on a flat d10 after a scene of hard use, or when the item says: a weapon after a jam, a cell after a full discharge, armour after a hit that got through.
  • Components have their own condition. A degraded optic on a nominal rifle degrades the optic's bonus, not the rifle's damage.
  • Swap: a component in better condition replaces one in worse, same family, no roll.
Why two channels and not one durability bar

A clogged filter and a punctured filter both stop working, but only one is a maintenance loop. Keeping wear and damage apart is what gives the tech character a job between fights, and it is why a degraded item adds signature: the worn servo whines, and maintaining it is a stealth decision.

What the engine needs

  • Items as objects with child components, saved by reference. Exists.
  • A slot as a property with a family tag, a feed list and an advisory capacity. Convention in the system.
  • Feeds, gates and tags matched by the system's prerequisite reporting. Needs the reason-in-words that the vision already lists as a gap.
  • A template constructor per item kind so the content tools can author them. Exists as a pattern.
  • Charges as a whole-number property spent in the cost step. Exists.
  • Signature parts as channel-tagged mods. The same small addition the signature rules need.
Open: what Degraded does to dice

"One step down" on a damage expression needs a rule: 2d6+2 to 2d6, or to 1d6+2? It should be the same step the margin bands use when they improve an effect, so the two are mirrors. Decide with the damage rules.

Open: vehicles and drones

The schema fits them, with crew stations as slots and the rigger's link as a feed. Whether a vehicle needs hit locations (the dropped "part" level) is not decided and is not needed for personal gear.

Open: resistances

The source model has a resistance profile per damage type on every piece. It is a damage-model concern and waits for the injury rules rather than being designed into items now.
