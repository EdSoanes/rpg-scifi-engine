System 04 · Core mechanics

Cooperation

Helpers pay in time. Divisible and indivisible tasks, who is qualified to help, what helping costs.

The principle

Help is time, not a bonus

A helper never adds to the lead's roll. A helper either does part of the work, or lends the lead their attention so the lead can work as if they had more time.

Why no "+2 for assistance"

A flat bonus for help inflates ratings, erodes the line between certain and uncertain tasks, and makes four amateurs the equal of one professional. Paying in time keeps the roll honest and makes helping cost the helper something real: their own action.

Two kinds of task

How help works
Task isExamplesA qualified helper
DivisibleResearch, searching a building, decrypting many files, patching up several wounded, loading a truckWorks in parallel. Makes their own progress roll each interval against the same work total. The task finishes sooner.
IndivisibleDefusing a device, one surgery, picking one lock, a sniper shot, a single negotiationLends attention. Shifts the lead's pace one step towards Careful at no extra time. The dice narrow; the clock does not move.
  • Every task says which kind it is. Most extended tasks are divisible. Most single-roll tasks are indivisible.
  • A divisible task takes any number of qualified helpers. Each one is a worker with their own pace and their own rolls.
  • An indivisible task takes one helper. A second pair of eyes helps; a crowd does not.
  • Lending attention cannot take the lead past Careful. Methodical is bought only with time.
  • Lending attention to a Rushed task makes it Normal. The spotter is what lets the sniper snap a good shot.

Who is qualified

  • A helper is qualified when their rating in the task's skill is within 2 of the lead's, or at least 3, whichever is lower.
  • A task may declare that anyone can help. Carrying boxes, holding a torch, keeping watch.
  • A task may declare that nobody can help. Some things are done alone.
  • A helper whose rating is higher than the lead's should lead instead. The sheet points this out and does not stop it.
Why within two

A novice watching an expert defuse a bomb catches nothing the expert would miss. A professional watching the same expert does. The threshold is loose enough that a competent team helps each other constantly, and tight enough that the decker cannot be talked through brain surgery.

What it costs the helper

  • In turn tracking, helping is an action. It costs the helper the task's base cost from the matching pool, at Normal pace, every turn they help. The face who talks the guard down is not the one covering the hacker.
  • Outside turn tracking, helping costs the helper the same intervals the lead spends. They are doing nothing else.
  • A helper who stops helping part way through a multi-turn or extended task leaves the lead at their own pace from the next payment or interval. Progress already made stands.

Opposition is not cooperation

  • Several characters attacking one target each roll their own task. There is no combined attack.
  • Several characters defending one position each react on their own points.
  • Where a group acts as one, such as a crew pushing a stalled vehicle, the task is divisible and the work total is set for it.
